# Hybrid Work Policy (version 2)

This version replaces the 2024 hybrid policy.

## Office days

- Employees work from the office 3 days per week.
- Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are the core office days for every team.

## Working from another city

- Up to 30 working days per year, with your manager's approval.
- Request at least 10 working days in advance and notify HR.

## Fully remote roles

A role can be fully remote only with director approval and a written business reason.

## Equipment

A one-time home office allowance of INR 15,000 is available after you complete 6 months of service. Submit receipts under the expense policy.
