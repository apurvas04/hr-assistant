# Travel and Expense Policy

## Domestic travel

- Air travel is economy class. Rail travel is AC 2-tier.
- Hotel limit: INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 per night elsewhere.
- Meal allowance: INR 1,200 per day while travelling.

## International travel

International travel needs prior approval from a VP. Book through the company travel desk.

## Claims

- Submit claims within 30 days of the expense, with receipts.
- Claims above INR 25,000 need director approval.
- Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 working days.

## Not reimbursable

Personal entertainment, fines, alcohol, and upgrades booked without approval are not reimbursable.
