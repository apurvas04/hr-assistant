# Hybrid Work Policy (version 1, archived)

This version is no longer in force. See the current Hybrid Work Policy.

## Office days

- Employees work from the office 2 days per week.
- Teams choose their own office days.

## Working from another city

- Up to 15 working days per year, with your manager's approval.

## Equipment

A one-time home office allowance of INR 10,000 is available after 12 months of service.
