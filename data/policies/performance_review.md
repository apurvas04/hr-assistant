# Performance Review Guide for Managers

## Cycle

- Reviews run twice a year, in April and October.
- Managers must submit reviews within 10 working days of the cycle opening.

## Rating scale

Ratings run from 1 (needs significant improvement) to 5 (outstanding).
Written feedback with specific examples is mandatory for ratings of 1 and 5.

## Calibration

- Ratings are calibrated across teams by HR and directors.
- Do not share calibration outcomes with your team before the HR communication date.

## Promotion

A promotion case needs two consecutive ratings of 4 or higher and a written recommendation from the manager.

## Performance improvement plan

A performance improvement plan runs for 60 days. HR must be involved before it starts.
