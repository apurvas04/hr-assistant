# Leave Policy

## Types of leave

- **Earned leave (EL):** 18 days per year, accrued at 1.5 days per month.
- **Casual leave (CL):** 8 days per year. A single request cannot exceed 3 consecutive days.
- **Sick leave (SL):** 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required if you are absent for more than 2 consecutive days.

## Carry forward and encashment

- Up to 10 unused EL days carry forward to the next year.
- CL and SL do not carry forward. Unused balances lapse on 31 March.
- At year end you may encash up to 5 EL days at your daily basic rate.

## Public holidays

The company observes 12 public holidays per year. The list is published every January.

## How to apply

- Apply through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance for planned leave of more than 2 days.
- Your manager approves or rejects within 2 working days.
- Sick leave can be applied for retroactively, within 3 working days of your return.

## Probation

Employees on probation (the first 6 months) accrue EL but can use only CL and SL until probation ends.
