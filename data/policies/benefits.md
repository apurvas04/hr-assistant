# Employee Benefits

## Health insurance

- Group health cover of INR 5,00,000 per family per year.
- Covers the employee, spouse and up to 2 children at no cost.
- Parents can be added through payroll deduction. The additional premium is paid by the employee.

## Life insurance

Group term life cover of 3 times annual fixed pay.

## Learning budget

- INR 30,000 per employee per year for courses, books and certifications.
- Needs manager approval before purchase.
- Submit the completion certificate to claim reimbursement.

## Wellness

Gym or fitness reimbursement up to INR 6,000 per year with receipts.

## Provident fund

The company contributes 12 percent of basic pay to the provident fund.
