# Grievance Process

## Raising a grievance

- Use the HR portal or email hr-grievance@merh.example.
- You can raise a grievance about your manager, a colleague, or a policy.

## What happens next

- HR acknowledges your grievance within 2 working days.
- The investigation is completed within 30 days.
- You are told the outcome in writing.

## Your protection

- Grievances are handled confidentially.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a grievance in good faith is prohibited.

## Harassment complaints

Complaints of harassment go to the Internal Committee, not through the normal grievance route. Email ic@merh.example.
