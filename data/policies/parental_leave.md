# Parental Leave Policy

Parental leave is separate from EL, CL and SL. It does not reduce your leave balances.

## Maternity leave

- 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children.
- 12 weeks of paid leave for a third child.
- Give at least 8 weeks of notice before the expected start date where possible.

## Paternity leave

- 10 working days of paid leave.
- It must be taken within 6 months of the birth of the child.

## Adoption leave

- 12 weeks of paid leave for the primary caregiver.
- Paternity leave rules apply to the other parent.

## Returning to work

Employees returning from maternity leave may work reduced hours for the first 4 weeks on request, with manager approval.
